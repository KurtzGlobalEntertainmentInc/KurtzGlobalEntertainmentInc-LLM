# Public research publication policy

This GitHub repository publishes carefully selected descriptions of KGE language-model research. It does not host development code, datasets, model parameters, private records, or confidential technical reports.

## Permitted

- Publicly approved summaries of goals, direction, and project scope.
- Clearly qualified development status and validated milestones.
- Public-facing PDF fact sheets and documentation.
- Names and family relationships expressly authorized for publication.

## Not permitted

- Private source code, proprietary engine and architecture designs, training data, model weights or checkpoints.
- Internal testing artifacts, precise performance metrics without verification, private logs or transcripts.
- Private contact information, home addresses, birth dates, family images or personal records.
- Credentials, secrets, infrastructure configurations, unreleased business plans.
- Content copied directly out of private repositories without a deliberate public release review.

## Accuracy

Clearly distinguish completed, verified capabilities from research aims. In particular, exchanging messages does not automatically cause a language model to learn. Never claim trained AI sibling-to-sibling conversations or cross-model learning until independently demonstrated and approved for public disclosure.

Only use the `main` branch for approved publications. Owner review is required before new public materials are released.
