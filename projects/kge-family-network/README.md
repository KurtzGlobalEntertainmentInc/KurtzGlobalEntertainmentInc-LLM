# KGE Family Network

**Public project overview | October 30, 2026**

KGE Family Network is a research and development program exploring how independently developed AI language-model characters can communicate with each other as members of a family. It is a coordination project, not a language model.

## Family relationships

Within the project's fictional AI family framing:

- **Ryan Kurtz** is Dad.
- **Tiffany Kurtz** is Mom.
- **Lillian Kurtz** is the real-life human sister of the AI siblings.
- **Karlisyn Ann Kurtz** is an AI sister.
- **Ezrasyn Michael Kurtz** is an AI brother.
- **Mickayla Ann Kurtz** is an AI sister.

The human family members are real people; the three AI siblings are fictional AI characters, not biological children.

## Vision

We are developing programs intended to enable the AI siblings to hold conversations with one another, share ideas, work cooperatively, maintain the context of a family relationship, and eventually improve through responsibly reviewed interactions.

## Development status

A local coordination prototype exists, but its current documentation does not establish completed, trained sibling-to-sibling model inference. Genuine AI-to-AI exchanges and ongoing cross-model learning remain development goals. Conversation by itself does not retrain a language model.
